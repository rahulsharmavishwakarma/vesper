# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users respectfully. Welcome different backgrounds and
experience levels, discuss technical decisions with evidence, and give feedback
about the work rather than the person. Respect requests to stop an interaction.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, and publishing
someone else's private information are unacceptable in project spaces. Never
share another person's recordings, transcripts, or credentials without consent.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful contributions, warn participants, or
restrict participation based on the severity and repetition of the behavior.
These expectations also apply to maintainers.

Report concerns privately to the repository owner using a private contact method
listed on their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/rahulsharmavishwakarma).
Do not include sensitive evidence in a public issue. Reports should be handled
discreetly and fairly, sharing information only as needed to address the concern.
