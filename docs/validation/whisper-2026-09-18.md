# Whisper native screen — 18 September 2026

**Whisper small and Turbo merit further qualification for English/French quality. This screen does not qualify production support or an uncontended latency improvement.** The existing Sherpa backend has completion, Hindi token-budget and noise-handling blockers. Cohere plus Qwen remains the selected default.

The [research and concrete integration proposal](../research-whisper.md) distinguishes primary papers, official capabilities, measured results and hypotheses. [Issue #45](https://github.com/rahulsharmavishwakarma/transcribe/issues/45) records scope and approval status. The transcript-free local debug report at `.cache/benchmarks/results/whisper-2026-09-18/debug-summary.json` contains per-case metrics/output hashes, artifact/source/executable identities, process failures and competing-work observations.

## Corpus and outcome

The complete suite produced **213/213 case records in 27 processes**, with no missing rows, process failures or logged native exceptions. A separate English repeat completed 45 cases with identical text, scores and errors, then was stopped while waiting for external inference to finish; its remaining 75 cases are explicitly not run. An earlier aborted attempt retained 53 complete/partial case records in the cache and contributes no timing conclusions. Repeated cases are not additional independent speech samples.

The shared English lane contains 30 cases/model: 24 natural FLEURS recordings, four synthetic meaning controls and two repeated reset recordings. Natural English has 610 reference words. Clips last 3.108–23.640 seconds. The natural subset comes from the existing public hard-regression corpus; no checkpoint tuning was performed. This is a small regression screen, not the complete FLEURS test set or a universal model ranking.

| Model / executed export | Natural English word edits | Normalized WER | Model files, decimal MB |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Current Cohere INT8 | 59 / 610 | 9.67% | See installed artifact hashes in JSON |
| Whisper tiny INT8 | 180 / 610 | 29.51% | 103.61 |
| Whisper small INT8 | 45 / 610 | 7.38% | 375.49 |
| Whisper Turbo INT8 | 30 / 610 | 4.92% | 1,036.61 |

WER uses the same Rust scorer as the production benchmark: Unicode normalization, case folding and punctuation/symbol separation. Grapheme error rates are retained separately. File size is not process RAM. These measurements apply to the named multilingual ONNX exports through this exact decoder; they do not rank whisper.cpp, PyTorch, faster-whisper or a different quantization.

The synthetic controls expose an important limitation of tiny: it lost the self-correction and final negation, producing an unrelated short instruction that the writing stage could not recover. Small, Turbo and Cohere preserved the correction and negation in that control. The spoken-instruction control triggered the existing writing fidelity rejection for all four models. English writing failures were retained: one each for Cohere/small/Turbo, six for tiny. There was one additional tiny writing rejection in the diagnostic lane. A lexical writing guard cannot validate words the ASR already misheard.

## Language, noise and duration gates

Each Whisper export ran four French and four Hindi cases in both explicit and automatic language modes. These are two natural recordings plus repetition/leading-silence diagnostics per language, not eight independent recordings.

| Export | French natural words, explicit / auto | Hindi natural words, explicit / auto |
| --- | --- | --- |
| tiny | 42 / 46 edits in each mode; 91.30% WER | 128 / 64 edits in each mode; 200% WER |
| small | 7 / 46; 15.22% | 56 / 64; 87.50% |
| Turbo | 4 / 46; 8.70% | 57 / 64; 89.06% |

The Hindi results fail qualification. Small and Turbo emitted 54 tokens for the 9.12-second natural recording and 82 for the 13.8-second one, consistent with reaching the inspected decoder's six-tokens-per-second cap. Native completion reason is unavailable, so this is evidence of a runtime restriction and a likely contributor, not proof that removing the cap will restore published model accuracy. The 29.36-second Hindi diagnostic was rejected by the probe's conservative 29-second boundary in all six applicable runs; its 100% WER remains recorded separately from the natural subset.

All three Whisper exports hallucinated text on **all three five-second natural-noise excerpts**. Insertions were 14 words for tiny, six for small and three for Turbo; Cohere inserted zero. There was no added VAD, denoiser, no-speech probability threshold or audio classifier. These results require a separately tested silence/noise policy before enabling an adapter. A VAD gate must also preserve quiet speech.

The quiet diagnostic repeats one natural utterance attenuated by 30 dB. Cohere returned empty text (100% WER); tiny had 75% WER, small and Turbo 33.33%. This narrow result does not establish general noise robustness or justify dropping quiet audio.

For each Whisper export, A / 180-second synthetic continuous input / A produced an explicit long-input rejection and identical A transcripts before/after. Together with the six Hindi boundary cases, **nine duration rejections** are preserved. This verifies the probe's guard and fresh-stream reset in those cases. It does not transcribe the full 180-second recording, qualify natural long-form speech, implement paced streaming, or measure first-partial/endpoint/stop-to-final latency. Code-switching and singing were not tested and remain hard qualification gaps.

## Observed timings — contention prevents an isolated ranking

The machine was Apple M5 Pro, 18 logical CPUs, 64 GiB RAM, **macOS 27.0 (26A428)**, battery power mode 0, CPU provider, eight native threads. This OS differs from the September 17 residency report, so Cohere was measured again. External compiler/native transcription jobs were detected despite sequential execution of this suite's own models. Nine of 27 processes had observed interference; every model had an affected English block. The partial English repeat also encountered contention. **Do not use these timings to claim a production latency improvement, regression or SOTA result.**

The following values are retained diagnostic observations from the complete suite, with the first request of each process excluded: 28 later requests/model, nearest-rank percentiles. Failed writing calls remain in timing distributions. These are ASR/optional-writing stages, excluding capture, audio-file setup, persistence, UI and insertion.

Whisper runs in an isolated native probe, while Cohere uses the production manager. Whisper ASR timing includes stream creation, feature acceptance, decode, result retrieval and stream release; artifact verification and recognizer load are separate once-per-process fields. Cohere ASR includes the manager call; its initial call includes verification/load. The comparable later-call subset excludes both first calls. Writer calls use the production manager with resident Qwen3.5-0.8B Q4_0, fresh context and prefix reuse disabled.

Cold measurements are not a distribution: in the first block, Whisper tiny/small/Turbo verification took 49/150/400ms and native load 116/422/512ms. Cohere's first ASR call took 2.756s. Its first writer call took 7.425s, whereas the first Whisper-associated writer calls took 0.858/0.950/0.926s. These outliers and the second-block starts remain in JSON; excluding starts from resident percentiles must not erase them from evidence. Filesystem caches were not flushed, and temperature, energy, sustained throttling and other devices were not measured.

## Reproduction and checks

`scripts/prepare-whisper-assets.py ROOT --download` explicitly fetches the pinned files into an ignored cache, verifies sizes/hashes, and writes per-model descriptors. Without `--download`, it only verifies existing files. Native artifact verification runs again before each probe process. Missing/changed audio is rejected and scored as a failed case; existing result files cannot be overwritten.

Prepare the public corpus using the hard-regression protocol. Build the evaluator and `whisper_probe` with the pinned native environment, then copy them into a **new** output directory's `binaries/` as `transcribe-eval` and `whisper-probe`. Record the actual environment in `environment.json` using the benchmark template. Complete downloads/builds before timed execution.

```sh
export DEVELOPER_DIR=/Library/Developer/CommandLineTools
source scripts/dev-env.sh
cargo build --release --locked -p transcribe-eval --example whisper_probe
cargo build --release --locked -p transcribe-eval

python3 scripts/qualify-whisper.py OUTPUT ARTIFACT_ROOT \
  .cache/benchmarks/results/audio-regression-2026-09-16 \
  .cache/benchmarks/datasets/long-asr-fleurs
```

`--english-only` runs the 120-case timing repeat in a separate output directory. The coordinator reverses model order between two 15-case blocks and records every process/row outcome. It watches known compiler/native processes once per second, waiting before a new job when they are active. The current runner stops after 120 seconds without a quiet slot, retains its plan and partial ledger, and captures its own source hash at startup. These bookkeeping improvements followed the recorded attempts; they do not change or retroactively qualify their timings. Monitoring cannot prove the absence of all short-lived/background activity.

For publication, retain `source.json`, `native-provenance.json` and `post-freeze-changes.json` alongside raw results; these are evidence snapshots, not a local task tracker. `scripts/summarize-whisper.py ROOT OUTPUT_JSON --repeat-root REPEAT_ROOT --excluded-root EARLIER_ATTEMPT` strips transcripts and personal paths, preserves missing/failing cases, and checks repeated text/score/error hashes. Raw evidence roots are `.cache/benchmarks/results/whisper-2026-09-18`, `whisper-2026-09-18-isolated` and `whisper-2026-09-18-english-repeat`; the word “isolated” in a directory name is not a measurement claim.

Verification completed: release builds, strict example Clippy, 17 evaluator library tests, Python compilation/Ruff, publisher artifact verification, model/audio checksum rejection, output-overwrite rejection, and all 213 main-suite case records. No production model selection, dependency, lifecycle or decoder behavior was changed. No files were staged or committed.
